## Overview

- **Company:** TVT BPO Co., Ltd.
- **Role:** E-commerce Operations Intern
- **Period:** Jun 2026 – Sep 2026
- **Platforms:** TikTok Shop, Shopee

## What I did

- **Product management:** listed new products and updated details, images and prices on both marketplaces.
- **Orders & inventory:** tracked order status and handled stock issues to avoid running out or mismatched quantities.
- **Complaints & returns:** worked with the warehouse to handle customer complaints and returns.
- **Customer care:** advised customers, collected feedback and proposed suitable solutions.

## What I learned

- How a store runs end to end, from listing a product to delivery and after-sales.
- The differences between TikTok Shop and Shopee in how products are shown and orders are handled.
- Working smoothly across operations, warehouse and customer care.
