## Overview

- **Type:** Graduation project — Management Information Systems, Banking University of Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Systems analyst
- **Period:** 07/2025 – 09/2025
- **Tools:** Draw.io, Figma, Visual Paradigm, Microsoft Visio, Canva

## What I did

- **Systems analysis & requirements:** gathered stakeholder needs and wrote the functional requirements specification for an online event ticketing system.
- **Process & data modeling:** designed **BPMN** (business process), **DFD** (data flow) and **ERD** (database) diagrams.
- **UI design:** designed the website interface in Figma, based on the analysis.

## Result

- Delivered a complete **systems analysis and design document** that meets the stakeholders' real requirements and is ready for development.

## What I learned

- How to turn user needs into clear requirements a development team can build from.
- Reading and modeling data with ERDs and DFDs — a foundation for working with and analyzing data.
