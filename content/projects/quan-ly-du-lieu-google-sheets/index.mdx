## Overview

- **Company:** TVT BPO Co., Ltd.
- **Role:** E-commerce Operations Intern
- **Tool:** Google Sheets

## What I did

- Updated and reconciled **SKU, price and inventory** data across marketplaces.
- Tracked **order status** to catch late, faulty or returned orders early.
- Kept the data clean and consistent so the whole operations team could rely on it.

## Why it matters

With a background in **Management Information Systems**, I enjoy working with data: accurate product and order data keeps a store from selling at the wrong price or running out unexpectedly, and lets the team respond to customers faster.
